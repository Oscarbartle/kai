**Kai now runs on Mac**, as well as Windows.

**New**
- **Price history.** Every item now has a chart at the bottom of its page: one line per product, with a dot each time prices were refreshed. Specials show as hollow dots, and you can hover a dot for the details.
- **Pack sizes up front.** Each product's size (500g, 750g, 1kg…) now shows as a badge next to its name everywhere, so similar products are easy to tell apart. Product names are tidied up too.
- **Choose a product for a recipe.** On a recipe's ingredient, tap the + to pick the exact product to buy for that recipe. It takes priority over your starred product when the recipe is added to a shopping list.

**Fixes**
- Mac: the Pantry loads properly and Woolworths sign-in works.
